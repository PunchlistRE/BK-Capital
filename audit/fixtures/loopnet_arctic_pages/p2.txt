Horvath & Tremblay is pleased to present the exclusive opportunity to purchase the mixed-use investment property
located at 743 Arctic Street in Bridgeport, CT (the “Property”). The three-story building contains three (3) Commercial
units, four (4) 2-Bedroom/1-Bathroom units, and four (4) 1-Bedroom/1-Bathroom units with 9,642 square feet of gross
living area in 13,392 square feet of gross area. The Property is situated upon a 0.12-acre parcel and has on-street parking.
743-751 Arctic Street is well-positioned in the East Side of Bridgeport, offering convenient access to the city’s downtown
business district, neighborhood retail corridors, and surrounding residential communities. The Property benefits from
excellent connectivity to major regional roadways, including State Street (Route 130), Interstate 95, and Route 8/Route 25,
providing efficient access throughout Bridgeport, Fairfield County, and the broader New Haven and New York
metropolitan areas. The East Side’s established residential character, proximity to employment centers, and access to
transportation infrastructure further enhance the Property’s appeal to renters seeking convenient access to employment,
retail, and regional amenities.
