Northeast Private Client Group is pleased to exclusively present 18 Fairfield Avenue, an 18-
unit multifamily building in Norwalk's South Norwalk (SoNo) neighborhood, near the South
Norwalk Metro-North station and the restaurants of Washington Street.
Built in 1967, the three-story brick building has 10,234 liveable square feet on a 0.48-acre lot
with 24 surface parking spaces. The unit mix is one (1) studio, sixteen (16) one-bedroom
apartments (four (4) standard, eleven (11) large and one (1) extra-large) and one (1) two-
bedroom.
The current owner has invested significantly in the property’s building systems and common
areas, including new heating and hot water systems with a full oil-to-natural-gas conversion,
upgraded lobby, entrance and hallways, and a renovated laundry room with new equipment.
Exterior improvements include siding repairs and a sealed parking lot. The roof was replaced
in August 2017, and the windows were replaced between 2011 and 2014.
The current owner has achieved significant rent increases through the common area
upgrades and a professional management strategy. He has not renovated any units. A new
owner has the opportunity to continue to increase the rent roll through unit level upgrades.
There is a renovation rent premium of about $400/unit on average across the 18 units, for an
additional $86,052 in gross rents.  O F F E R I N G S U M M A R Y
