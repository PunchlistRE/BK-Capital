HIGHWAY ACCESS
I-84 crosses Hartford toward Waterbury and New York State in one
direction and the Massachusetts Turnpike toward Boston in the
other, and meets I-91 downtown, the route between New Haven
and Springfield. Route 2 leads into eastern Connecticut, and Route
9 runs from New Britain to Middletown.
ECONOMIC ANCHORS
Hartford's insurers, Travelers, The Hartford and Aetna, anchor downtown
employment, and East Hartford, home to Pratt & Whitney's engine
works, is about 11 minutes by car. Up I-91, Springfield adds Baystate
Health and MassMutual, and New Haven carries Yale and Yale New
Haven Health.
