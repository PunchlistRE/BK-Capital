Greater Hartford Overview
Greater Hartford is Connecticut's capital region, with about 897,000 residents in
Hartford County and roughly 1.17 million across the metro area. Insurance, health
care and aerospace anchor its economy. I-84 and I-91 cross downtown, the Hartford
Line carries rail service to New Haven and Springfield, and Bradley International
Airport serves the region from Windsor Locks.

M A J O R A R E A E M P L O Y E R S
Insurance & Financial Services
The Cigna Group is headquartered in Bloomfield, alongside the Hartford insurers
Travelers, The Hartford, Aetna and HSB.
Health Care & Education
Hartford HealthCare runs Hartford Hospital, Trinity Health Of New England runs Saint
Francis Hospital, and Connecticut Children's is downtown, with UConn Health in
Farmington, Trinity College in Hartford and the University of Hartford in West Hartford.
Aerospace & Industry
Pratt & Whitney builds jet engines in East Hartford, Collins Aerospace operates in
Windsor Locks, Otis Worldwide is headquartered in Farmington, Stanley Black & Decker
in New Britain, and Eversource Energy serves the region from Hartford and Berlin.
