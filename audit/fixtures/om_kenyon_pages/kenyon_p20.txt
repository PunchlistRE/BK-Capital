Hartford Overview
Connecticut's capital, where I-84 meets I-91 and the Hartford
Line
Hartford is Connecticut's capital, a city of about 121,000 residents at the
center of a metro area of roughly 1.17 million, and about two-thirds of its
households rent. 37 Kenyon Street sits in the West End, on the city's
western edge along Farmington Avenue.
I-84 and I-91 meet downtown. Hartford Union Station carries CTrail
Hartford Line and Amtrak trains to New Haven and Springfield and is the
eastern end of CTfastrak, the busway to New Britain, which stops at
Sigourney Street less than a mile from the property.
Insurance and health care are the city's largest employers. Travelers and
The Hartford are headquartered downtown, Aetna, part of CVS Health,
runs its headquarters campus on Farmington Avenue, and Hartford
HealthCare operates Hartford Hospital, the system's flagship.
