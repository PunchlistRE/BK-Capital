509-515 Gregory Street Bridgeport, CT
PROPERTY DESCRIPTION
Baldwin Pearson & Co., Inc. is pleased to present 509–515 Gregory Street, Bridgeport, Connecticut, a compelling six-unit multifamily
investment opportunity offering attractive in-place cash flow and significant rental upside. Ideally positioned on a prominent corner at
Gregory and Rennell Streets in Bridgeport’s South Side, this approximately 5,700-square-foot property presents investors with the
opportunity to acquire a well-positioned residential asset in an excellent neighborhood in one of the fastest growing rental markets in the US.
The property consists of six generously sized apartments, including four 2.5-bedroom units and two 2-bedroom units. These larger-than-
average layouts appeal to a broad tenant base and offer a competitive advantage in attracting and retaining residents. All utilities are
separately metered, helping minimize owner-paid operating expenses, while a dedicated rear parking area accommodates approximately six
vehicles.
The property's location further enhances its appeal. Situated within walking distance of Seaside Park, the University of Bridgeport, and
Downtown Bridgeport, the property benefits from proximity to some of the city's most significant neighborhood amenities and employment
centers. These nearby destinations support consistent rental demand from students, university personnel, and working professionals.
Beyond its attractive in-place income, the property offers a clear path to increased revenue. One apartment is currently vacant and available
for immediate lease-up, while several existing units are leased at rents below current market levels. This creates an opportunity for a new
owner to enhance the property's income through strategic leasing and rent adjustments as units turn over, with the potential to realize
meaningful upside without requiring substantial upfront capital investment.
