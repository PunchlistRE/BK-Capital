Summary of investment

Harbor Point Residences is a 120-unit multifamily ground-up development in Stamford, CT of about 132,000 SF. The plan is to buy the site for $7.00M, build over 24 months, lease up over 17 months to stabilisation in month 43, and sell in month 48. Total cost is $44.64M ($372,009 a unit), funded by $16.57M of equity (37.00%) and a $28.07M construction loan at 7.25% (63.00% of cost). There is no permanent takeout: the $28.07M construction loan stays outstanding after stabilisation and is repaid from the sale proceeds at month 48.

In the Base case the deal returns 33.09% levered and 2.71x ($28.69M of profit on $16.57M of equity), and 22.32% before financing: 21.1 points above the 12.00% hurdle. The return rests on a 8.91% stabilised yield on cost against a 5.00% exit cap (391 bps of development spread), and 94.00% of what equity gets back arrives at the sale. It stays above the hurdle if the exit cap widens by up to 208 bps, or rents fall 23.00%, or hard costs overrun by 56.00%, one at a time.

Model checks: 0 failed, 2 warnings, 21 passed, 0 not applicable; to know: interest accrues beyond the loan commitment; costs exceed what equity plus the commitment can fund. Credit gate: CONDITIONAL. WHAT THIS MODEL CANNOT YET DO
• Costs are entered as 13 hard-cost trades and 16 soft-cost lines; lines in a brief are merged to fit, and each cannot be escalated or reforecast on its own.
• Contingency and initial reserves are not tracked or released as separate balances.
• The construction loan is sized by an LTC formula; a fixed commitment, a facility ceiling, an equity-first threshold and an unused-commitment fee cannot be entered.
• The GP catch-up is a share-of-profit target (100.00% until the GP holds that share), not a catch-up rate; the preferred return is shared by LP and GP unless hurdles are tested on LP flows.
• A refinance is illustrated at stabilisation; the property is still sold at the end of the hold.
• Source citations, extraction confidence and broker-versus-model discrepancy reporting are not part of this summary.
