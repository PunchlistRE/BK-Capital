ⓘ
Underwritten value is not the purchase price. It is Year-1 NOI capitalised at the exit cap - $3,594,877 ÷ 5.00% = $71,897,536 - which is how a lender sizes against value rather than against what you paid. On this deal the two differ by $64,897,536, so an LTV percentage and an LTC percentage of the same number give different loans. The loan is sized at the lowest of the three constraints above. If you mean a percentage of what the deal costs you, that is Max LTC - note that it is measured against the total cost basis (price plus closing costs plus acquisition fee = $7,350,000 here, not the $7,000,000 price), so 65.00% of basis is only 65.00% of price when there are no acquisition costs. Bad debt / credit loss is now editable above, next to vacancy. It defaults to 0.50% and it does affect NOI, so it was reducing revenue with no way to see or change it.
