The cover, every footer and the PDF title carry the fixture identifier, and the brief's cover lists the unconfirmed terms as assumptions pending deal-team confirmation (`fixture_labels.js`).

The LP-only preferred return alone moves the LP IRR by 1.56 points and the GP IRR by -6.90 points, so the LP and GP figures above are **not definitive** until the deal team confirms it.

A 50% catch-up rate and a 100% rate give the same LP and GP totals here: the rate changes how much is paid in the catch-up tier and how much in the next tier, but the GP reaches its 20% share in the same month either way. The rate would matter for a deal whose distributions end inside the catch-up tier.

## 5. The retired branch's 32.30%

Measured on the retired branch's own build (not reproducible from `main`, kept for the record): its Harbor result was 32.30% levered, LP 26.25%, GP 67.46%, promote $6.37M. Measured differences from the current brief fixture:

- **Interest capitalised through the sale** (the brief's term; not in the current engine): worth +0.19 points on that branch (32.12% without it, 32.30% with it). The same convention priced by the independent model on the current fixture is in section 3.
- **Equity first.** The retired branch paid the loan fee and unused fee from equity *outside* the $14.7M; the current engine counts them *inside* it (equity $15.08M against $15.15M).
- **Reserves.** The retired branch used four buckets (the general and insurance/tax buckets could pay lease-up shortfalls); the current fixture enters three, two of them held. With no reserves the current fixture is 33.50%.
- **Preferred return and catch-up.** The retired branch returned capital pro rata and then paid the pref to the LP only; the current engine pays the LP its whole account (capital and pref) first. The LP IRR is 26.25% there against 27.92% here for the same words in the brief.
The retired branch's decomposition is not complete: after the items above a residual of about 0.2 to 0.5 points remains between the two builds that I did not isolate (the two engines differ in several small timing conventions). It is not attributed to anything here.
